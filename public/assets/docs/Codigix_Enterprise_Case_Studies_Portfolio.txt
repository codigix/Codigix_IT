CASE STUDY 1: SMART MANUFACTURING ERP + IIOT INTEGRATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Client: Sterling Techno Systems
Industry: Precision Manufacturing & Industrial Equipment
Duration: 6 Months
Tech Stack: Node.js, React, MySQL, IIoT Sensors, MQTT, InfluxDB

Business Challenge:
Sterling Techno System relied on manual production logs and disconnected department software, leading to high machine downtime, human data entry errors, and lack of real-time OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) visibility across shopfloor production lines.

Codigix Solution:

- Rolled out automated 3-way purchase order matching and role-based inventory alerts.
